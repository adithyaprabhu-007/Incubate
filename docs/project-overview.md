What is EmergencyDPI?

EmergencyDPI is a secure digital emergency health identity designed to help authorized doctors and emergency responders quickly access critical patient information during medical emergencies.

In situations where a patient may be unconscious or unable to communicate, EmergencyDPI enables responders to identify the patient and request essential information through a controlled access process. Patients can control what information is shared whenever consent is possible, while access can be limited, time-bound, and recorded for transparency.

The system focuses on balancing rapid access to critical medical information with patient privacy and security.

Core Flow

Verify → Request → Authorize → Share → Expire → Audit

EmergencyDPI aims to make emergency information accessible when needed, controlled when shared, and accountable after access.
